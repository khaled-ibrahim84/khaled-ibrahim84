# Product Portfolio

This folder contains sanitized, original case studies based on product problems I have worked around in fintech, payments and enterprise delivery. The documents demonstrate product thinking and prototyping: they are not employer systems, production code or claims that these concepts were deployed exactly as shown.

## How to review

Each case study follows the same structure: problem, users, product principles, workflow, rules and controls, success measures, delivery plan and explicit non-goals. This makes the work useful to Product, Engineering, Compliance, Finance and Operations reviewers.

| Case study | Primary lens |
|---|---|
| [Merchant Pricing Configuration](merchant-pricing-configuration/) | Translating commercial terms into controlled rules |
| [Merchant Onboarding & KYB](merchant-onboarding-kyb/) | Balancing conversion, compliance and operations |
| [Agentic AI Lead Recovery](agentic-ai-lead-recovery/) | Safe AI autonomy and human handover |
| [Payments & Payouts Orchestration](payments-payouts-orchestration/) | Provider abstraction and transaction reliability |

## Confidentiality

Names, values, flows and examples are illustrative. No customer data, credentials, production endpoints, proprietary source code or confidential employer/vendor information is included.
